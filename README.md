# 5 Useful Sources for Human Resource Management (HRM)

These resources are collected for learning and understanding Human Resource Management concepts, including recruitment, selection, training, employee engagement, performance management and HR strategies.

## 1. International Labour Organization (ILO)
- Website: https://www.ilo.org/
- Focus Areas: HR Management, Labour Relations, Employee Welfare and Working Conditions.

## 2. Society for Human Resource Management (SHRM)
- Website: https://www.shrm.org/
- Focus Areas: Recruitment, Talent Acquisition, Employee Engagement and HR Strategies.

## 3. Chartered Institute of Personnel and Development (CIPD)
- Website: https://www.cipd.org/
- Focus Areas: Learning and Development, Employee Retention, Performance Management and HR Practices.

## 4. Department of Personnel and Training (DoPT), Government of India
- Website: https://dopt.gov.in/
- Focus Areas: Recruitment, Training, Competency-Based HRM and Employee Performance.

## 5. International Organization for Standardization (ISO)
- Website: https://www.iso.org/
- Focus Areas: Human Resource Management Standards, Workforce Planning and Employee Engagement.

## Learning Objectives
- Understand the fundamentals of HRM.
- Learn recruitment and selection practices.
- Explore training and development methods.
- Understand employee engagement and retention.
- Study modern HR strategies and standards.

**Purpose:** These sources are useful for MBA HRM students, academic assignments, research and practical HR knowledge.
